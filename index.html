How the cases are made

Checked, not guessed.

Sourced

Every name, number and date is checked against court records, regulatory filings, company disclosures and government reports. Sources are listed with each video.

Fair

Only people convicted, or who pleaded guilty, are named. Charges are reported as charges. Victims are never named.

No playbooks

We show what happened and why it worked, never how to repeat it. The point of every case is the control that stops the next one.
